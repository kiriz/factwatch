## Claim Text

"Humans only use 10% of their brain capacity — the other 90% remains dormant and untapped."

## Context

One of the most persistent myths in popular science. Cited in self-help books, movies (Lucy, Limitless), and advertising for cognitive supplements. Despite decades of neuroscience research, the claim refuses to die.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check neuroimaging studies (fMRI, PET). Verify whether any region of the brain is truly inactive. Look at what happens to brain tissue when it's genuinely unused (it atrophies). Check scientific consensus from neuroscience organizations.
