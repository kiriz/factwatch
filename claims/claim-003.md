## Claim Text

"mRNA COVID-19 vaccines alter or permanently change human DNA."

## Context

One of the most widely circulated vaccine misinformation claims during the COVID-19 pandemic. Disputed by virologists, molecular biologists, and major health agencies worldwide.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Consult peer-reviewed molecular biology literature. Check CDC, WHO, and NIH statements. Key mechanism to verify: mRNA cannot be reverse-transcribed into DNA without reverse transcriptase enzyme. Check if any credible peer-reviewed evidence to the contrary has emerged.
