## Claim Text

"Arctic sea ice has declined significantly since satellite records began in 1979."

## Context

Frequently cited in climate discussions. NASA and NSIDC maintain continuous satellite records from 1979. Often disputed by climate skeptics who point to short-term recovery years.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check NSIDC, NASA, and NOAA data. Look at September minimum extent as the primary metric. Verify trend is statistically significant, not just year-to-year variability.
