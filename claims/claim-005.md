## Claim Text

"The US Congress has not passed a complete federal budget on time since 1997."

## Context

Often cited to illustrate Congressional dysfunction. The claim hinges on the definition of "on time" (before October 1, the start of the fiscal year) and "complete" (all 12 appropriations bills passed before the deadline).

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check Congressional Research Service records. Fiscal year starts October 1. Verify if any year between 1998 and present had all 12 appropriations bills enacted before the deadline. Note distinction between omnibus packages and individual bills.
