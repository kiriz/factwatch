## Claim Text

"Bitcoin's annual electricity consumption exceeds that of entire countries like Argentina."

## Context

Widely cited in climate and energy debates. Cambridge University maintains the Cambridge Bitcoin Electricity Consumption Index (CBECI). The comparison shifts as Bitcoin mining difficulty, price, and energy sources change. Critics argue renewable energy share is growing.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check the Cambridge CBECI for current figures. Compare to IEA national electricity consumption data. Verify whether the comparison holds with current mining conditions. Note whether the claim refers to TWh/year.
