## Claim Text

"In 2016, Google DeepMind's AlphaGo defeated Lee Sedol — the world's top Go player — 4 games to 1, a result experts said was a decade ahead of schedule."

## Context

Considered a watershed moment in AI history, comparable to Deep Blue vs Kasparov in chess. Go was believed to be too complex for computers due to its search space of 10^170 possible positions. The win shocked the AI research community and changed timelines for AGI predictions.
