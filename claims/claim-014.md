## Claim Text

"The Moon is slowly moving away from Earth at a rate of about 3.8 centimeters per year."

## Context

Measured using retroreflectors left by Apollo astronauts via Lunar Laser Ranging experiments. Has implications for the long-term future of Earth's tides and axial stability. A rare case of a precise, continuously measured astronomical fact most people don't know.
