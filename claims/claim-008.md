## Claim Text

"Octopuses have three hearts and their blood is blue."

## Context

Sounds like science fiction but is widely cited in marine biology education. The blue color comes from hemocyanin rather than hemoglobin. This claim is a great example of truth being stranger than fiction.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check marine biology sources. Verify the function of each heart (two branchial hearts, one systemic). Confirm the copper-based hemocyanin mechanism that makes blood blue. Check whether the blood turns colorless when deoxygenated.
