## Claim Text

"The United States national debt has surpassed $34 trillion."

## Context

Widely cited in political and economic commentary. The US Treasury publishes real-time debt figures. Claim became prominent in early 2024 when the debt first crossed this threshold.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check US Treasury Fiscal Data (fiscaldata.treasury.gov). Note distinction between gross debt and debt held by the public. Verify current figure — may have grown beyond $34T by time of verification.
