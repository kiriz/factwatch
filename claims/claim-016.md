## Claim Text

"Generative AI will replace 300 million jobs worldwide."

## Context

This figure traces to a March 2023 Goldman Sachs report, "The Potentially Large Effects of Artificial Intelligence on Economic Growth." The "300 million jobs" number is repeated constantly in AI-and-work coverage, usually framed as outright replacement. The original report's language — jobs *exposed to* automation, with AI *complementing* many roles rather than eliminating them — is frequently lost. A high-value claim to verify precisely because the gap between what was said and what is repeated is large.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check the primary source: the Goldman Sachs Global Economics Analyst (Hatzius et al., 26 March 2023). Verify the exact wording — the report estimates roughly 300 million full-time jobs could be "exposed to automation," and that ~two-thirds of occupations could see *some* automation, not full replacement. Distinguish "exposed to," "complemented by," and "replaced by." Note the report also projected AI could raise global GDP. Cross-check with later labour-economics commentary (e.g. MIT, Brookings) on automation creating as well as displacing jobs. Expected nuance: the bare "will replace 300 million jobs" framing overstates the source.
