## Claim Text

"The Great Wall of China is the only man-made structure visible from space with the naked eye."

## Context

Appears in textbooks worldwide and was repeated for decades as fact. Chinese astronaut Yang Liwei attempted to see it from orbit in 2003 and could not. NASA has weighed in. Yet the myth persists in curricula.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check astronaut accounts, NASA statements, and optical physics. The wall is roughly 9 meters wide — compare to road widths. Verify what actually IS visible from low Earth orbit with the naked eye.
