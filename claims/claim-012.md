## Claim Text

"Lightning never strikes the same place twice."

## Context

This saying is so common it's become a figure of speech for unlikely events repeating. The Empire State Building and other tall structures provide real-world data. Used to justify risk decisions that may be fatally misguided.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check National Weather Service and NOAA data on lightning strike patterns. Verify how many times the Empire State Building is struck per year. Explain the physics of why tall conductors are repeatedly targeted.
