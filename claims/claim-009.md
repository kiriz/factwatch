## Claim Text

"Napoleon Bonaparte was unusually short — around 5 feet 2 inches tall."

## Context

The "Napoleon complex" — aggressive behavior to compensate for short stature — is named after him. The myth largely originated from British wartime propaganda and a confusion between French and English inches. His actual height is contested.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Check historical records of Napoleon's height in French inches vs English inches. Verify average male height in early 19th century France. Look at accounts from contemporaries and his physician's records. The British caricaturist James Gillray's role in perpetuating the myth is worth examining.
