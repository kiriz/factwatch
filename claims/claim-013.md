## Claim Text

"Social media use causes depression and anxiety in teenagers."

## Context

One of the most politically and scientifically contested claims of the 2020s. Jonathan Haidt's "The Anxious Generation" argues causation strongly. Others, including Amy Orben and Andrew Przybylski, argue the effect size is tiny. Governments are legislating on this. The science is genuinely contested.

## Verification Notes (Human)

Look for meta-analyses and systematic reviews. Check both Haidt's research and critics like Orben/Przybylski. Verify whether studies show correlation vs causation. Check whether effect sizes are practically significant. Note the difference between passive consumption and active engagement.
